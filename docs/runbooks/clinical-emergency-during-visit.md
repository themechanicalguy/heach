# Clinical emergency during a home visit (amendment A17)

- **Status:** Draft for owner review (30 October 2026). Proposed by the home-care plan, accepted as part of the core amendments; the wording needs clinical and legal review before the Stage A pilot.
- **Applies to:** physiotherapists, nurses, attendants and sample collectors during a visit or shift; support staff handling the resulting ticket.

## In the patient's home

1. **Call 108** (emergency ambulance) straight away. Stay with the patient and follow the
   dispatcher's instructions. Do not start any procedure that is not on the prescription.
2. **Tell the family** or the person who booked, if they are not present.
3. In the app, open the booking and press **Report a safety concern**, topic *Medical
   emergency*. This raises a **P0 ticket** and alerts operations at once (HS-06).
4. When it is safe, record what happened in the visit notes (what was done, what was seen,
   advice). Never put health details in SMS or chat outside the app (SE-09).

## Support (within 15 minutes of a P0 ticket)

1. Assign the ticket to yourself in the admin **Tickets** section.
2. Call the provider, then the booker, using the contact in the bookings console.
3. If you need the ticket's description, use **Open private details** (break-glass) with the
   reason *Safety investigation*. The grant lasts 30 minutes and the patient sees it (SE-03,
   SE-06).
4. Add internal notes as you go; resolve the ticket with the outcome. If the provider acted
   against this runbook, open a review (AD-03) before any further booking.

## After the event

- Cancel or reassign the provider's later bookings for the patient if care must change.
- Refunds follow the booking's state; a manual refund needs finance (PY-02).
- Record lessons in the follow-up register if a process change is needed.
