The one list of checks and actions that are still due for Steps 1 and 2, with who closes each, when,
how, and what it blocks. Other pages (the [acceptance matrix](step-2-acceptance-matrix.md), the
[Step 2 log](step-2-progress.md), [implementation status](implementation-status.md)) link here
instead of keeping their own lists. The findings behind each row are in the
[Step 1 and Step 2 audit](step-1-2-audit.md) of 25 October 2026.

- **Created:** 25 October 2026 (audit). Update a row when it closes: set State to Closed with the date
  and a link to the evidence. Never close a row before its trigger date, and never close a
  scheduled-observation row with a manual run.
- **Times:** UTC unless marked IST (India, UTC+05:30). The dev host runs on UTC and its timers name
  UTC explicitly; the AWS credit reminders are scheduled in `Asia/Kolkata`. Weekdays checked with
  the system calendar on 25 October 2026.
- **Owner** means the account holder (repository owner). Claude Code may prepare records and run
  read-only checks, but never marks a human review, approval or acceptance itself.
